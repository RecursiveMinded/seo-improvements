# SEO strategy

Source: Muse research report, 2026-11-24 (10 engines, Nov 2026), with Claude second pass.

## Program rules
1. **Revenue intent first.** Prioritize queries and pages closest to money.
2. **One wedge per project.** Google plus 1-2 sector channels; no spreading thin.
3. **Shared infra once.** Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools + IndexNow, schema markup, master checklist (`CHECKLIST.md`), set up the same way for every project.
4. **No paid tools, no gray-hat.**
5. **Measure against money.** The Personal SEO toolkit digest (separate project) feeds the weekly loop.

## Tiers
- **Tier 1 (revenue nearest):** US Notary Journal (usnotaryjournal.com), RouteBlue (routeblue.app), Black Card Rejected
- **Tier 2 (pre-launch SEO):** TwinNests (twinnests.com), Cardenda
- **Tier 3 (shared infra only):** wedding-qrcode.com, Atlanta's Public Notary, Spaceman, Bonnett Life, ScanMingle, WeekSimmer, Le'Pour ATL

## Sequence
1. Foundation pass on Tier 1 and Tier 2 (`CHECKLIST.md`).
2. Google post-core-update pass. The Nov 10-24 2026 core update rewards people-first content and E-E-A-T. Compare a full post-update week in GSC before reacting.
3. Sector channel pass (per-project wedges in `projects/`).
4. Bing AI-preference migration, done before **2027-03-01** (Bing change announced Oct 30 2026, effective Feb 1 2027).
5. Weekly digest loop.
