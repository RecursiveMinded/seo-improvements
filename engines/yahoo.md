# Yahoo

## Current rules
- Yahoo organic results are powered by Bing. Optimize for Bing (`bing.md`).
- Console: Bing Webmaster Tools (no separate Yahoo console). Yahoo's own help directs site owners to Bing Webmaster Tools for submission.
- No paid ranking: you can't pay for higher organic placement.

Verified: 2026-11-24 by Muse.

## Recent changes
- **2026-11-10:** Yahoo Scout exited beta and now underpins all of Yahoo Search (Pivot Search: traditional results plus Scout AI answers, US/UK/Europe/Switzerland). Source: Yahoo Inc press release, 2026-11-10: https://www.yahooinc.com/press/after-delivering-more-than-3-billion-answers-yahoo-scout-exits-beta-to-underpin-all-of-yahoo-search
  - Does Scout affect visibility? It adds an AI answer layer on top of the Bing-powered results; the press release announces no change to the crawler, index or traditional ranking, and does not end Bing supplying traditional results. Scout answers carry prominent source links, so the Bing GEO format (one topic per URL, explicit verifiable facts near the top, consistent entity names) is also the Yahoo AI-visibility play. No separate Yahoo action beyond Bing.
  - Yahoo's release calls Yahoo Search the third largest search engine in the US and says Scout delivered 3B+ answers since Q1 2026.

Verified: 2026-11-24 by Muse.
